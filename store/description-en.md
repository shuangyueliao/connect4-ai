# Connect Four AI — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Line up four in a row against a real minimax AI. Three difficulty levels, from casual Easy to full-depth Hard. The classic Connect Four duel, free in your browser.

## Details

- Genre: Strategy / Board / Classic
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (tap a column to drop). Keyboard: ←/→ aim, ↓ or Space to drop.
- Features: 7×6 board vs minimax AI (alpha-beta pruning; Easy depth-1,
  Normal depth-4, Hard depth-6 with move ordering), first-move choice
  (You / AI / Random), per-difficulty win/loss/draw records, gravity drop
  animation, glowing neon discs, win-line highlight, procedural graphics
  & sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio, minimax + alpha-beta.
  No frameworks, no tracking.
